Privacy Policy

PlenoCare - Developed by Rootkore

Effective Date: November 9, 2026

Last Updated: November 9, 2026

1. Introduction

Rootkore ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored, and processed when you use the PlenoCare mobile application ("the App").

PlenoCare is a business application designed for authorized staff members of organizations to manage customer support, tickets, conversations, emails, and related activities.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the App, we may process the following categories of information:

  • Account information: Email address, password during authentication, name, job title, department, user ID, and organizational role.
  • Device information: A randomly generated installation identifier, device platform, and application version for authentication and device identification.
  • Support content: Ticket messages, replies, email conversations, customer records, WhatsApp conversations, feedback, and related support information made available through your organization.
  • Attachments: Files and images that authorized users choose to upload or share through the App.

3. How We Use Information

Information is processed for purposes including:

  • Authenticating authorized staff members.
  • Maintaining user sessions and account access.
  • Displaying tickets, customer records, and conversations.
  • Sending replies and managing customer support activities.
  • Uploading and viewing attachments.
  • Providing password recovery functionality.
  • Maintaining and improving the functionality and security of the App.

4. Customer Information and Organizational Data

PlenoCare allows authorized staff members to access and process information belonging to their organization's customers. This may include customer names, contact details, tickets, emails, chat messages, WhatsApp conversations, attachments, and feedback.

This information is processed on behalf of the organization using PlenoCare. The organization is responsible for determining the purposes for which its customer information is processed and managing appropriate access to that information.

End customers who have questions or requests concerning information managed by an organization should contact that organization directly.

5. Data Storage and Security

Information transmitted between the App and our backend services is sent over HTTPS.

Certain account information, authentication tokens, organizational information, and device identifiers are stored locally using encrypted secure storage.

Passwords are not intentionally stored persistently by the App.

Temporary files and cached images may be stored on the device to support viewing and downloading content. Downloaded files may also be saved to a location selected by the user.

We take reasonable measures to protect information. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

Information may be transmitted to our backend services to provide the App's functionality.

Customer communications may involve third-party communication providers configured by the organization through its support systems.

The App may display remote images included in email content. Loading these images may disclose technical information, such as your IP address, to the server hosting the image.

Links opened from the App may direct you to external websites or applications, including browsers, email applications, maps, telephone, and SMS applications. Those services operate under their own privacy policies.

We do not use the App for advertising or behavioral advertising tracking.

7. Data Retention

Account and customer-support information is retained according to the applicable organization's requirements, service arrangements, and operational needs.

Information stored locally may remain on the device until it is cleared, the session is removed, or the App is uninstalled. Temporary and cached files may be removed by the operating system or application.

For questions about the retention or deletion of organizational customer records, please contact your organization administrator.

8. Account Access and Deletion Requests

PlenoCare accounts are created and managed by the relevant organization administrator.

If you are a staff member and wish to deactivate or request deletion of your account, please contact your organization administrator.

The administrator or organization is responsible for handling account access and deletion requests according to its applicable policies and service arrangements.

Logging out removes the locally stored authentication session and associated profile information from the device. Logging out does not itself delete server-side account or customer-support records.

9. Children's Privacy

PlenoCare is a business application intended for authorized organizational staff members. It is not designed or intended to be used by children.

We do not knowingly direct the App to children or intentionally collect personal information from children through the App.

10. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable laws, you may have rights concerning access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information.

Staff members should contact their organization administrator regarding account information and organizational records. You may also contact us using the details below for privacy-related questions.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when necessary to reflect changes in the App, our practices, or applicable legal requirements.

Any updates will be published on this page, together with the revised effective date.
